About

What this site is for

Work Field Notes is an independent reference site covering the practical questions that come up mid-task: what a role actually involves, which body has authority, what a system is for, and where the numbers come from.

Why it exists

Search for something like "health information professionals" and you get a professional association page, a university admissions page, a job board, and a government statistics table. Each is accurate. None of them answers the whole question, and none of them tells you which parts have changed recently.

This site fills that gap. Each guide takes one question and answers it completely — the definition, the numbers, the routes in, the things that commonly trip people up — and cites where each fact came from.

What is covered

Eleven sections, listed on the home page. They range across health information, financial services regulation, cybersecurity, management systems, business services and web infrastructure. The common thread is not an industry; it is the reader. These are the things working professionals in the United States look up while doing their job.

How guides are researched and written

  • Primary sources first: regulators, statistical agencies, professional bodies and standards organisations, linked directly.
  • Figures carry a date. A salary band or an enforcement total is a fact about a period, not a permanent truth.
  • The answer goes in the first paragraph. Context follows for readers who want it.
  • Guides are revised when the underlying source changes. The revision date appears at the top of each page.
  • Where sources disagree, the disagreement is stated rather than smoothed over.

Editorial independence

No company pays to appear on this site, and no coverage decision is sold. Where a product or vendor is named, it is because it is genuinely part of the answer.

How the site is funded

Work Field Notes is supported by display advertising. Advertisers have no input into what is covered or what is said. Details of the data collected by advertising partners are in the Privacy Policy.

Not professional advice. This site publishes reference material. It is not legal, medical, financial or compliance advice, and it is not a substitute for a qualified professional who knows your situation.

Corrections

If something here is wrong or out of date, tell us and it will be fixed and the change noted. The contact page has the details.
