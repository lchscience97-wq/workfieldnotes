Cloud & Storage

Storage decisions look like a price comparison and rarely are. Retrieval cost, egress, redundancy and access latency usually matter more than the headline rate.

This section covers storage options and what separates them, how data centres are built and located, and the factors worth weighing before committing to a provider.
