Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026. This policy explains what Work Field Notes collects, who else receives data when you visit, and how to control it.

Who runs this site

Work Field Notes is an independent publication reachable at hello@workfieldnotes.com. That address is also the contact point for any question about this policy or about data held on you.

What we collect directly

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run a newsletter or a comment system. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

If you email us, we hold that message and your address for as long as needed to deal with it.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. This site uses them in two ways.

  • Measurement. Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits and understand navigation. This data is aggregated.
  • Advertising. Google and other vendors use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads and limit how often you see the same one.

Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of a wider set of vendors at aboutads.info and Your Online Choices. Third-party vendors and ad networks that appear on this site may be listed at Google's partner sites page.

If we add other advertising partners, this section will be updated with their names and their own privacy policies.

Analytics

Google Analytics processes data on our behalf. You can prevent it collecting your visits with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Google's own handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to processing, or to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Residents of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom hold these rights under the GDPR; residents of California hold comparable rights under the CCPA and CPRA.

We do not sell personal information for money. To exercise any right, email hello@workfieldnotes.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Controlling cookies yourself

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking them will not stop you reading anything here, though some advertising may become less relevant.

External links

Guides link out to regulators, agencies, standards bodies and vendors. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for how other sites handle your data.

Data retention

Analytics data is retained for the period set in our Google Analytics configuration and is aggregated. Email correspondence is kept only while it is needed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes will be flagged on the home page.
