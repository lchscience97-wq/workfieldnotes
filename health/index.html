Health Information & Careers

Health information sits between clinical care and administration. The people who manage it code diagnoses into billable form, audit documentation for completeness, control who may release a record, and keep the systems holding all of it accurate and secure.

This section covers what those roles involve day to day, which credentials employers actually ask for, what the work pays across the United States, and how people move between coding, analysis, compliance and management. Where figures come from federal statistics or professional bodies, they are cited with the year they were published.
