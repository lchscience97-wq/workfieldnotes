Business Services

Most businesses buy services they only need occasionally — transport, cleaning, disposal, specialist inspection. Finding and comparing those providers is a recurring problem with no obvious reference.

This section covers how to find, evaluate and hire the service companies a business needs, what the pricing structures usually look like, and which questions separate a competent provider from a plausible one.
