How this is written

Every guide answers its question in the first paragraph. Sources are named and linked. Figures carry the date they were published, because a salary band or a penalty total from three years ago is not the same fact as this year's.

Nothing here is legal, medical or financial advice. It is reference material for people who then go and do the work.
