Cards & Payments

Card products are priced, marketed and regulated in ways that rarely appear in the marketing copy. Balances, transfers, approval criteria and fee structures follow rules that are public but scattered.

This section explains how card products actually work, what the terms mean, and where the numbers come from. It does not recommend products or issuers. Anything involving your own credit position is a decision for you and, where the stakes justify it, a qualified adviser.
