Financial Services & Regulation

Financial regulation is jurisdictional. The same activity can sit under different authorities depending on where it happens, and bodies with similar names often have nothing in common beyond the name.

This section maps who regulates what, how authorisation works in practice, and where the common confusions lie — including bodies that no longer exist but still appear in policy documents. Enforcement figures carry the period they cover, because a penalty total from three years ago is a different fact from this year's.
